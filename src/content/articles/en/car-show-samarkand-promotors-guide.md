A car show in Samarkand is no longer just a row of polished cars in a car park. PROMOTORS SHOW SAMARKAND, held on 12–13 September 2026 at SOF EXPO Samarkand, combined a drift championship, car audio and tuning contests, a car exhibition and a trade expo of parts, chemicals and service equipment. This guide explains how the format works for car owners, clubs, automotive brands and visitors, and how to prepare for the next edition.

## What the PROMOTORS car show in Samarkand includes

The festival has two halves that run at the same time. Outside, on the 5 000 m² show area, there is the track, the pit zone and the market. Inside, the 4 400 m² hall hosts the car exhibition and the expo of brands: spare parts, detailing, car audio, car wash equipment and chemicals.

The 2026 programme included the following.

- A drift championship for rear-wheel-drive cars with heats on the central track and awards on stage.
- SPL car audio and tuning in two classes, rookies and professionals, judged on body, interior, engine bay and multimedia.
- A retro car exhibition and a display of tuned and premium cars in the hall.
- Non-motorized racing for teams of up to four people with cars under 80 kg.
- A car parade through the streets of Samarkand, a motorcycle show and prize draws on the main stage.
- A pit zone and market selling parts, accessories, chemicals and merchandise.

The total prize fund was 20 million UZS. In non-motorized racing it was split into 10 million for the Grand Prix, 6 million for design and 4 million for team uniforms.

## How to get your car into an auto show in Uzbekistan

For an owner or a club, the route into the show starts with an application, not with arriving on the day. In 2026, drift entries went through the organisers' Telegram manager, and a drift entry was free for the car owner once the organisers approved it.

### Drift

The technical minimum for the drift championship was clear: a rear-wheel-drive car, a hydraulic handbrake, and a suit and helmet for the driver. Prepare photos of the car, a short description of its build and your driving experience before you apply.

### Exhibition and tuning classes

For the car exhibition and the SPL tuning contest, choose the class honestly. A rookie entry with a clean, finished build is judged better than an unfinished professional one. Bring the vehicle documents if you enter a show class.

### Sponsor logos on your car

Any sponsor integration on a participant's car must be approved by the organisers. If a local shop or brand supports your build, agree the livery with the organisers before you print the wrap.

## Automotive event sponsorship and brand activation

For a brand, a dealer, a tuning studio or an oils and accessories company, the question is not whether people will come but how to turn a crowd into contacts and sales. In 2026 there were three formats.

- Hall expo: a 9–18 m² stand with a product showcase and a checkout zone; selling from the stand was allowed.
- Outdoor pit stop: a working bay from 36 m² next to the track, with power and water.
- Show sponsorship: branding of the track, stage, awards and ticket zone, host mentions and a promo zone at the entrance.

### Planning the activation

1. Set one measurable goal, for example a number of service-station contacts, product sales or test-drive bookings.
2. Choose the format that shows the product working: chemicals and films on real cars, audio and lighting switched on, not stored on a shelf.
3. If you plan test drives or demos on spectators' cars, agree the route, timing and safety rules with the organisers in advance.
4. Plan content: the drift track, the parade and the stage produce photo and video material that partners can use for months.
5. Capture leads on the spot with a short form or a QR code, and tag each contact as a service owner, distributor or retail customer.

The B2B side of the 2026 show was about orders: distributors of consumables and wash equipment negotiated with service and wash owners at the stand, while the show programme kept visitors in the expo area all day.

## Safety and logistics at a drift show in Samarkand

A drift show combines speed, noise and a crowd, so the rules are strict and apply to everyone. The track-side area is restricted, drivers wear suits and helmets, and the organisers set sound limits and approve car livery. Participants should follow every briefing and keep their cars in the assigned zones.

For exhibitors, plan load-in and load-out times, the delivery of cars and trailers, and the power you need; the venue provides up to 700 kW. Outdoor pit stops have power and water, which matters for car wash and detailing demonstrations. Check insurance for your cars and equipment with your insurer before the event.

## What visitors should know

- In 2026 tickets were sold through Ticketon.uz, and trade specialists from the automotive sector received free passes.
- A ticket covers all festival zones except pit stops and working areas.
- Non-motorized racing needs a separate entry and a helmet.
- Children under 14 come with an adult.

Arrive early for the drift heats, because the best viewing spots around the track fill first, and plan time for the hall, where the car exhibition and the brand expo run through the day.
