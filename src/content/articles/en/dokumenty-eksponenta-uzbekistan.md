### What you can show and what you can bring

An exhibition is a public demonstration, and most samples travel under a simplified import regime: goods arrive as exhibits, rarely sell and leave again. Sectoral requirements are not cancelled, and they are strictest for food, drinks, agrochemistry and pressure equipment.

#### Basic pack for an exhibitor

* the organizer's registration form and the participation contract;
* invoice and packing list for the exhibits (non-commercial);
* product documents: an Uzbek certificate or declaration of conformity, or test protocols where certification is still planned;
* for food: quality certificate, shelf life, composition, labelling in Uzbek and Russian;
* tasting permission, arranged through the organizer together with sanitary rules for the area;
* for machinery: safety passports, connection diagrams, confirmed power draw.

#### Labelling, the most common stumble

Uzbek labelling rules cover language, importer details, production date and storage conditions. A demo unit can travel without final packaging, but price lists and handouts are marketing material and follow the same language rules.

#### Tastings and taking payment

Tastings run only in the approved area, with water, crockery and trained staff. If you plan to take payment at the stand — common at consumer-facing shows — settle acquiring and fiscal documents in advance through your Uzbek entity or a local distributor, not on the spot.

#### Is a contract signed at a show valid

Yes. A commercial contract or letter of intent signed at an exhibition is binding, but check three things: jurisdiction and language, delivery terms and Incoterms, and certification requirements for the batch. It is sensible to state that the supply obligation starts once product conformity is confirmed.

#### How a local partner lowers risk

Exhibitors arriving with a distributor get certification for the portfolio, a warehouse, service and customer payment terms. Organizers welcome it: a joint stand is cheaper and buyers trust a supplier with local support.
