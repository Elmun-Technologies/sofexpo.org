### White label in Uzbek practice

The factory produces with its own technology, the seller puts its trademark on it. For a marketplace seller this raises margin and ends dependence on someone else's price list; for the factory it loads the line and creates a stable order without marketing spend.

Uzbekistan is ready: local production of cosmetics, household chemistry, confectionery, packaging and food semi-finished products is growing, while marketplace demand outpaces the ability of producers to package, certify and publish a product card.

#### Where a seller starts

1. Niche and unit economics: cost, logistics, marketplace commission, ad budget, break-even.
2. Product requirements: composition, volume, packaging, shelf life, label language.
3. Factory search: trade shows, exhibitor catalogues, references.
4. Sample and audit: pilot batch, defect terms, production schedule.
5. Documents: certification, declaration, rights to the brand and packaging design.
6. Contract: minimum order, price, payment terms, channel exclusivity.

#### What the factory cares about

Producers look at three things: order volume, payment reliability and whether the seller will undercut the brand. Negotiations are won by whoever brings a promotion plan and a realistic forecast, not by whoever asks for a lower price.
