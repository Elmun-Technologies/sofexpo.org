### Dates and season logic

**AGROPRO EXPO 2027** runs **2–4 March 2027** at SOF EXPO Samarkand. March is deliberate: it is when farms and clusters buy machinery, seeds, irrigation and agrochemistry before the field season.

#### What the audience looks for

* agricultural machinery: tractors, harvesters, forage equipment, attachments, spare parts;
* irrigation: drip, pivot and drum systems, pumping stations, watering automation;
* agrochemistry and laboratories: fertilizers, plant protection, soil diagnostics;
* seeds and seedlings, greenhouses and hotbeds;
* storage and logistics: fruit stores, cold chambers, packaging;
* livestock and poultry, standardization and certification;
* finance: banks and leasing companies with machinery programmes.

#### The market

Uzbekistan agriculture produces over USD 25 billion of output, 90.3% of it outside the state sector, with 85,000+ farms and 468 agro-clusters (146 fruit and vegetable, 157 grain, 122 cotton-textile). The state subsidises machinery and irrigation purchases and opens preferential credit lines — the exhibition is where suppliers meet the farms that use these programmes.

#### AGROPRO EXPO 2026 results

100+ companies and 4,500+ specialists from the CIS, with participants from Russia, Türkiye, the Netherlands and Germany.
