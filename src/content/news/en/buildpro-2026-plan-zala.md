### The 2026 expo structure

**BUILD PRO EXPO 2026** runs **9–11 November 2026** at SOF EXPO Samarkand as the fifth international construction exhibition of the centre. The expo is divided into ten zones: building materials; tools and fasteners; machine tools and equipment; house building; real estate; landscape and greening; interior and design; light and electricity; windows, doors and facades; ceramics and stone.

The split is not a formality — it decides who physically walks past your stand. A visitor choosing a facade and a visitor choosing a contractor follow different routes, so we agree the location around your commercial task.

#### Previous results

2025: 1,600+ visitors, 80+ exhibiting companies, 10+ forums and sessions. Participants came from Uzbekistan, Russia, Türkiye and China.

#### How to pick a location

1. Set the goal: dealer network, projects, retail sales or market entry.
2. Choose the section and the adjacent zones where your buyer already is.
3. Submit a request — the manager sends the live floor plan with free spots and the per-m² rate.
4. Agree the build: ready-made Octanorm or a custom structure from your brand book.

Oversized machinery and facade samples go to the 5,000 m² open area, with truck access and crane unloading.
