### What is open

Applications are being accepted for **FOODERA EXPO 2026**, the specialised food and beverage exhibition in Uzbekistan, held **20–22 October 2026** at SOF EXPO Samarkand — 4,400 m² of indoor expo and 5,000 m² of open ground.

FOODERA connects manufacturers, distributors, importers and suppliers with retail chains, wholesale, HoReCa and catering buyers. For a producer it is the shortest route in the region from a stand tasting to a signed supply contract.

#### Participation formats

| Format | Area | Included |
|---|---|---|
| Standard stand | 9 m² | shell scheme, branding, furniture, power, two badges |
| Premium stand | 18 m² | priority location, larger advertising surface, meeting counter |
| Raw space | 36 m²+ | space only, build to your own design |

**38 premium stands** remain. Locations are fixed by contract, not by verbal booking — in previous editions the spots near the entrance and the stage sold out 6–8 weeks ahead.

#### Thematic sections

Grocery, soft drinks, tea and coffee, confectionery and bakery, dairy and cheese, meat, poultry and eggs, frozen and semi-finished products, preserves, oils, fats and sauces, gastronomic delicacies, organic and healthy food, packaging and equipment.

#### Business programme

Blind tastings for category managers, pre-matched 15-minute buying meetings, the Food Retail forum on shelves, pricing and private label, and the Best Product contest with awards on day one.
