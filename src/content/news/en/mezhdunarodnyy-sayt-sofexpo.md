### What changed

SOF EXPO Samarkand has published its international website. The goal is simple: give a foreign company as much decision-grade information as a local one has.

#### What is new

* two languages, Russian and English, with every page addressed individually;
* a dedicated **organizers** section: hall rental, rates, technical sheet, readiness checklist;
* **exhibitor** pages: packages, stand construction, equipment rental, advertising surfaces, catalogue;
* **visitor** pages: registration, tickets, programme, travel and hotels;
* an archive of past editions with verified numbers, catalogues and photo reports, so attendance can be checked rather than promised;
* machine-readable event data (schema.org/Event) so exhibitions appear in rich results and third-party calendars.

#### Why it matters

For an exporter, joining an exhibition abroad is a transparency question: area, power, ceiling height, price, logistics, documents. Without these on the site, no application appears. Now every parameter is public.
